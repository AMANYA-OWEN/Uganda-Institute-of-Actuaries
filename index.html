The Actuarial Profession in Uganda

Setting the standard for actuarial practice, education and public trust.

The Institute of Actuaries of Uganda is the recognised professional body for actuaries, upholding rigorous examination standards and a binding code of conduct across insurance, pensions, banking and public policy.

What We Do

A profession organised around rigour and the public interest

Examine & Certify

We set and mark a four-stage professional examination, from foundational mathematics to specialist practice, leading to Associateship and Fellowship.

View the pathway →

Set Standards

We issue Actuarial Standards of Practice covering reserving, capital, pensions valuation and reporting, binding on all members in public practice.

Read our standards →

Protect the Public

Through a mandatory code of conduct, disciplinary process and continuing education requirement, we safeguard the integrity of actuarial advice.
