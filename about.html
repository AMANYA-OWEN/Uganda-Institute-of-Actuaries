Who We Are

Established by law, governed in the public interest.

The Institute of Actuaries of Uganda is the body corporate created under the Actuaries Act, Cap. 214, responsible for the education, regulation and discipline of actuaries practising in Uganda.

Our Mandate

A statutory profession, not a voluntary association

Founded in 2009, the Institute exists to protect the public interest wherever actuarial judgement bears on financial promises made over the long term — in insurance contracts, pension schemes, and the solvency of financial institutions. Membership is earned through a rigorous, four-stage professional examination and maintained through continuing professional development and adherence to a binding code of conduct.

The Institute works closely with the Insurance Regulatory Authority, Bank of Uganda and National Social Security Fund to ensure that actuarial opinions relied upon by regulators, boards and the public meet a consistent, defensible standard.

“Our duty runs first to the public who rely on actuarial certainty, not to the institution that pays the actuary's fee.”
Governance

Council

Professional Discipline

Code of Conduct

Every member — Student, Associate or Fellow — is bound by the Institute's Code of Conduct, covering integrity, competence, confidentiality, and the duty to speak up when actuarial advice is being misused. Breaches are investigated by an independent Disciplinary Committee, whose findings may result in censure, suspension or removal from the register.

Founding Legislation

The Actuaries Act, Cap. 214, establishes the Institute as the sole body empowered to register actuaries in Uganda and to set the qualifying examination.
